Looking ahead

Career Goals

My current primary career goal is to pursue an internship in software development or cybersecurity for the summer of 2028. I hope to gain experience working on real projects and learn how to apply what I have learned in class in a professional environment. An internship would help me discover what direction best fits my interests for my career.

To prepare for this, I will strengthen my programming skills with my coursework and through continued personal coding projects. I want to build on my experience with Java and JavaScript and learn other coding languages. I plan to explore cybersecurity with projects and practice exercises. These experiences will help me learn more about these fields and identify what skills I want to develop further.

I will continue updating my résumé and portfolio to reflect all of my experience and projects. I will attend career fairs, research employers I am interested in, and practice technical interviews. This preparation will enable me to explain what I have done clearly and feel confident when I apply for internships.

My longer-term career goal is to use all these experiences to find what career path I want to pursue. I do not yet have a specific job title in mind, but I want to continue learning and find work that will help me discover my interests.
